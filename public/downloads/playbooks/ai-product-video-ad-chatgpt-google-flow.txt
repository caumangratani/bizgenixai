How to make a product video ad with ChatGPT and Google Flow
Bizgenix AI playbook · checked 2026-11-28 · https://bizgenix.ai/playbooks/ai-product-video-ad-chatgpt-google-flow

What you need
- One clean photo of your product.
- ChatGPT, to write the video prompt.
- Google Flow, Google's AI video tool, to generate the clip.
- Optional: your brand name, offer and audience.


Step 1: Take a clean product photo
Use one front-facing image: plain background if possible, product clearly visible, good light, no blur, no collage.


Step 2: Ask ChatGPT for the video prompt
Upload the photo to ChatGPT and paste this:

I want to turn this product image into a premium 8-second AI video ad.
Analyze the product image and create a Google Flow video prompt for a cinematic product commercial.
The output should include:
1) Product analysis
Identify:
- what the product is
- likely target audience
- visual style that would suit it
- premium emotions / brand vibe it should communicate
2) Ad concept
Create 3 different ad angles for this product:
- Luxury / premium
- Problem-solution / performance
- Aesthetic / viral social ad
3) Final Google Flow prompt
Write one polished Google Flow video prompt for the strongest ad concept.
The prompt should include:
- camera movement
- lighting
- environment / background
- product motion
- cinematic details
- texture / reflections / atmosphere
- premium ad style
- vertical 9:16 framing for social media
4) Shot direction
Give me a short shot breakdown:
- opening shot
- hero close-up
- motion / reveal shot
- ending brand shot
Keep the final video prompt clean and ready to paste into Google Flow.


Step 3: Pick one angle
- Luxury: perfumes, skincare, watches, jewellery, fashion.
- Problem and solution: gadgets, kitchen tools, supplements, utility products.
- Social: trend-led products and reels.


Step 4: Generate in Google Flow
1. Open Google Flow and start a new project.
2. Choose Video.
3. Upload your product photo as the reference image.
4. Paste the prompt ChatGPT wrote.
5. Set the aspect ratio to 9:16 for reels and Shorts, and a length of 6 to 8 seconds.

Menu names in Flow change from time to time; look for the option that adds an image as a reference or start frame.


Step 5: Make several versions
Generate three to five versions with small changes: stronger lighting, a different camera move, a cleaner hero shot, more atmosphere. The quality comes from this iteration, not from one prompt.


Step 6: Judge the result
1. Product clarity: can you see the product clearly, and is it your product, with the right label and shape?
2. Feel: does it look like an advertisement or like random AI footage?
3. Motion: is the camera move smooth?
4. Brand fit: would you post it under your brand name?

If any answer is no, generate again. Add your text and call to action in your normal video editor.


The prompt formula
[product] + [setting] + [lighting] + [camera movement] + [texture details] + [brand feel] + [aspect ratio]


Three example prompts
A luxury perfume bottle on a glossy black reflective surface, golden rim lighting, floating mist, slow cinematic push-in, premium reflections, ultra-detailed glass texture, high-end commercial look, 9:16 vertical ad
A premium skincare serum bottle on wet stone with soft water droplets, clean beauty ad aesthetic, gentle camera orbit, bright luxury lighting, minimal background, realistic product texture, 9:16 vertical
A premium sneaker floating above a dark textured floor, dynamic side lighting, slow rotation, dust particles, sporty cinematic ad look, crisp product details, bold contrast, 9:16 social ad framing


Before you run it as a paid ad
AI video can change a label, a logo or the shape of a product. Check every frame against the real product, and do not show a result or feature the product does not have.
