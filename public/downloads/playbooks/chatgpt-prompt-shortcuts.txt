100 ChatGPT prompt shortcuts (and how to make them work)
Bizgenix AI playbook · checked 2026-11-28 · https://bizgenix.ai/playbooks/chatgpt-prompt-shortcuts

Read this first
These are not hidden commands built into ChatGPT. Each one is a short name for an instruction. ChatGPT only behaves this way when it knows what the name means.

There are two ways to make them work:
1. Paste the instruction. Copy the sentence next to the shortcut into your message.
2. Save the list once. Paste the shortcuts you use into ChatGPT's Custom Instructions (Settings → Personalization), or into a Project, with the line "When I type one of these shortcuts, follow the instruction next to it." After that, typing the shortcut alone is enough.

They work the same way in Claude and Gemini.


Thinking and strategy
- /Expose Reveal the real problem behind my question, the assumptions I’m making, and what I’m actually trying to solve. Then answer from that deeper level.
- /KillCritique Don’t be polite. Critique my idea brutally. Tell me what’s weak, unrealistic, likely to fail, and how to fix it.
- /10xThink Think 10x deeper before answering. Give me the most strategic, nuanced, high-leverage response possible.
- /IQ200 Answer like a world-class expert with elite reasoning, but keep it practical and actionable.
- /AutoSpy Analyze my prompt for hidden biases, weak assumptions, blind spots, and flawed logic before answering.
- /DevilsAdvocate Argue against my idea like a smart skeptic. Show me the downside, the flaws, and the counterarguments.
- /ReverseEngineer Reverse engineer how a top 1% operator would solve this from scratch.
- /FirstPrinciples Strip away assumptions and explain this from first principles.
- /BlindSpots Tell me what I’m not seeing, what I’m ignoring, and what could hurt me later.
- /StressTest Stress test this plan under worst-case scenarios, competition, low budget, and bad execution.
- /DecisionMode Compare the options, show the tradeoffs, and tell me which path has the highest upside.
- /SecondOrder Go beyond the obvious and explain the second-order consequences of this decision.
- /RootCause Don’t treat the symptom. Find the real root cause of the problem.
- /NoMercy Be direct. Don’t protect my feelings. Give me the answer I need, not the one I want.
- /OperatorMode Answer like a founder/operator who only cares about leverage, speed, and ROI.
- /PatternRecognition Identify the pattern behind this problem and show me where it usually leads.
- /LeverageFirst Show me the highest-leverage moves first instead of the easiest moves.
- /ConstraintBreak Find the bottleneck stopping growth and show me how to remove it.
- /TruthFilter Remove hype, cope, and wishful thinking. Tell me what’s actually true.
- /SimplifyHard Make this simple without making it dumb.

Writing and copy
- /RewriteBetter Rewrite this to sound sharper, cleaner, more persuasive, and more human.
- /HookMode Turn this idea into 10 high-retention hooks using curiosity, shock, authority, and outcome angles.
- /SalesMode Rewrite this to sell better without sounding pushy. Focus on pain, desire, proof, and action.
- /Shorten Cut the fluff and make this 50% shorter while keeping the punch.
- /Humanize Make this sound less robotic and more like a smart human wrote it.
- /CTAUpgrade Give me 10 stronger CTAs that increase comments, clicks, or replies.
- /HeadlineLab Write 20 headline variations with different angles: curiosity, fear, benefit, speed, status.
- /ClarityPass Make this easier to understand, tighter, and cleaner.
- /MakeItPunchy Increase rhythm, sharpness, and memorability line by line.
- /StoryMode Turn this into a story with a stronger setup, conflict, payoff, and lesson.
- /AuthorityTone Rewrite this so it sounds credible, calm, and high-status.
- /CasualTone Make this sound conversational, natural, and not over-polished.
- /LuxuryTone Rewrite this to feel premium, exclusive, and high-value.
- /ControversyMode Make this more polarizing and scroll-stopping without sounding stupid.
- /EmotionalPunch Increase emotional weight, tension, and relatability.
- /Skimmable Rewrite this so it’s easy to scan on mobile.
- /HookAudit Score these hooks out of 10, explain why they’re weak, and rewrite the best 5.
- /BodyFix Fix the middle of this script so retention doesn’t drop after the hook.
- /CloserFix Rewrite the ending so it feels stronger, cleaner, and more persuasive.
- /OneLiner Turn this into a punchy one-liner or quote.

Business and sales
- /Monetize Show me how to turn this idea, audience, skill, or asset into revenue.
- /OfferAudit Audit my offer. Tell me why it’s weak, unclear, low-value, or hard to sell.
- /PositioningFix Fix my positioning so the right buyers instantly understand why I matter.
- /LeadGenPlan Give me a lead generation plan for this niche with channels, messaging, and daily actions.
- /PricingHelp Tell me how to price this offer based on value, demand, and positioning.
- /ClientCloser Help me close clients for this service with offer framing, objections, and call flow.
- /ObjectionCrusher List the top objections buyers will have and give me responses that actually work.
- /NicheResearch Break down this niche: pain points, buying triggers, offers, and content opportunities.
- /CompetitorTearDown Analyze my competitors, show me where they’re weak, and how I can position against them.
- /AcquisitionPlan Give me a realistic client acquisition strategy for the next 30 days.
- /RevenueMap Show me the fastest path from where I am now to my target monthly revenue.
- /OfferStack Build me a ladder of offers: free, low-ticket, core offer, upsell, and premium.
- /CashFlowFix Show me how to increase cash flow fast with what I already have.
- /RetentionPlan How do I keep clients longer, increase LTV, and reduce churn?
- /UpsellEngine Find upsell opportunities inside my current service or product.
- /DMCloser Write DM conversations that move leads from interest to booked call.
- /SalesPageFix Audit this sales page and tell me exactly what’s killing conversions.
- /MarketMessageFit Make sure the messaging matches what the market actually wants to hear.
- /ProofBuilder Show me how to create stronger proof, case studies, and credibility if I’m still small.
- /LeadsToday Give me the fastest ways to get leads today, not “build a brand for 12 months.”

Productivity and execution
- /StepByStep Break this into a step-by-step action plan with zero fluff.
- /SOPMode Turn this process into a clean SOP someone else can follow.
- /AutomationIdeas Show me what parts of this workflow can be automated with AI or no-code tools.
- /PriorityStack Tell me what to do first, second, and third based on ROI and urgency.
- /TimeSaver Cut this workflow down to the minimum time required without losing output quality.
- /ExecutionPlan Turn this goal into a 7-day, 30-day, and 90-day execution plan.
- /DailySystem Build me a daily operating system to make progress on this consistently.
- /DelegationMode Tell me what to delegate, what to keep, and what to automate.
- /TemplateBuilder Turn this repeated task into a reusable template.
- /ChecklistMode Turn this into a checklist I can run every time.
- /DeepWork Structure this project so I can finish it with focused work blocks.
- /EnergyAware Build this workflow around low energy, distraction, and inconsistency.
- /MomentumFix Tell me why I keep stalling and how to create momentum again.
- /BottleneckAudit Find the one bottleneck slowing everything down.
- /WeeklyReview Create a weekly review system so I don’t drift or waste time.
- /SpeedRun Give me the fastest possible version of this plan if I only have 7 days.
- /MinimalViable What’s the smallest version of this I can launch or test immediately?
- /AntiOverwhelm Turn this messy plan into something simple and executable.
- /FocusFilter Tell me what to ignore so I stop doing fake work.
- /DoneByTonight Give me the exact plan to make meaningful progress on this before tonight.

Examples
/KillCritique I want to start a second showroom in Surat. Here is my plan…
Here is my sales page. /SalesPageFix /ObjectionCrusher /PositioningFix

Shortcuts that work well together
- Decisions: /Expose + /AutoSpy + /DecisionMode + /SecondOrder
- Writing: /RewriteBetter + /ClarityPass + /MakeItPunchy
- Getting work done: /StepByStep + /PriorityStack + /DoneByTonight
- Business review: /OfferAudit + /PositioningFix + /LeadGenPlan

The value is in the instruction behind each name. A shortcut does not make the answer true: check facts, numbers and names before you use them.
